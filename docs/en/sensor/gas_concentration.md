# Gas Concentration Sensors

Gas concentration sensors are used to detect and measure the amount of a specific gas (or gases) present in the surrounding air, usually represented in Parts Per Million (PPM) or as a percentage of total volume.  
They are useful as environmental watchdogs across a variety of applications, ranging from safety and industrial automation to tracking indoor and outdoor pollution levels.

Gasses that are commonly detected include: O2, CO, H2S, NH3, H2, O3, SO2, NO2, HCL, CL2, HF and PH3.
